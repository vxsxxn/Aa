The whole trick

"The cat sat on the ___" → "mat". That's the entire skill. Trained on a huge amount of text, the guess gets scarily accurate — and accuracy at that scale looks like thinking.

Why it lies confidently: it's guessing the next word, not checking facts. A confident guess is still just a guess.

Ties back to Lesson 1: the "mind" you feel in chat is texture in the word choices. Impressive texture — but texture.
