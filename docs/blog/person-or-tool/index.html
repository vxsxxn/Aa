Why it feels like a person

  you type: "hi, what can you do?"
        │
        ▼
  ┌───────────────────────┐        the whole machine:
  │ words in → words out  │        no memory, no eyes, no self.
  └───────────────────────┘        just text going in,
        │                          words being picked,
        ▼                          text coming out.
  "Hey! I'd love to help."
        │
        ▼
  ┌────────────┐
  │   person   │   ← your brain has ONE program for
  └────────────┘     fluent language, and it says "person".
   what you feel     you can't switch it off — you also
                     yell at video games.

Your brain has exactly one program for fluent language, and it says "person". You can't turn it off — you also get angry at video games.

The friendly wording is a design choice, not a sign of life. A calculator could say "I'd love to". It doesn't, so nobody hugs one.

Takeaway: judge it like any tool — by what it does, not how it talks. Verifying its answers is normal use, not rudeness.
