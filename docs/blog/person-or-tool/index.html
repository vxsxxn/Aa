Why it feels like a person

Your brain has exactly one program for fluent language, and it says "person". You can't turn it off — you also get angry at video games.

The wording on the right is a design choice, not a sign of life. A calculator could say "I'd love to". It doesn't, so nobody hugs one.

Takeaway: judge it like any tool — by what it does, not how it talks. Verifying its answers is normal use, not rudeness.
